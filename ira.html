The Journey

How Ira Converts Property Enquiries Into Site Visits

From the first message to the morning sales brief, Ira manages the complete lead journey so your team never loses a serious buyer to slow follow-up.

STEP 01 — Instant Lead Capture

Ira starts the conversation before the lead goes cold.

When a buyer enquires through WhatsApp, Instagram, your website, a property portal, or a lead ad, Ira captures the enquiry instantly and begins a natural conversation within seconds.

Every enquiry gets an instant first response.
STEP 02 — Buyer Intent Mapping

Ira understands what the buyer is really looking for.

Ira identifies budget, location preference, configuration, possession timeline, urgency, and buying intent, then builds a clean lead profile for your sales team.

Your team knows buyer intent before the first call.
STEP 03 — Smart Follow-Up Engine

Ira follows up without sounding repetitive.

Most buyers need multiple touchpoints before they book a visit. Ira sends timed, relevant follow-ups across WhatsApp, SMS, and email based on how the buyer responds.

Follow-ups keep running even when your team is busy.
STEP 04 — Site Visit Booking

Ira turns interest into confirmed visits.

When a buyer is ready, Ira shares available time slots, confirms the visit, updates the calendar, sends reminders, and gives the sales rep full context before the meeting.

More serious buyers become scheduled site visits.
STEP 05 — Daily Sales Brief

Ira gives your team the day before the day starts.

Every morning, Ira sends a crisp action brief showing hot leads, site visits, overnight replies, follow-ups due, and buyers who need a human call.

Sales reps start the day with clarity.
